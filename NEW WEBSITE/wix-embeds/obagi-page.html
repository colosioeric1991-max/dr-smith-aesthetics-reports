About Obagi Medical

Founded by Dr Zein Obagi, a board-certified dermatologist with over 35 years of clinical research, Obagi Medical is one of the world's leading medical-grade skincare brands, trusted by dermatologists and aesthetic practitioners worldwide.

Unlike high-street skincare, Obagi formulations are not available over the counter. They are exclusively prescribed through licensed medical professionals, ensuring every product is matched to the right patient and skin type. This is what makes them genuinely effective.

Obagi works at a cellular level, targeting the root causes of skin concerns rather than masking them. The range addresses pigmentation, uneven skin tone, ageing, acne and overall skin health, using prescription-strength ingredients including tretinoin, hydroquinone and vitamin C. The results are visible, measurable and long-lasting.

Why Dr Smith recommends Obagi

As a medically trained doctor, Dr Smith believes that great skin starts from within. Obagi's science-led approach aligns perfectly with this philosophy, combining prescription-grade ingredients with clinical rigour to deliver real, lasting improvements.

Dr Smith is an authorised Obagi practitioner, which means he can prescribe the full range including tretinoin-based products that are simply not available anywhere else. Every recommendation is made following a thorough skin consultation and assessment, so you can be confident the products you receive are right for your skin.

Obagi also works beautifully alongside injectable treatments, maintaining and enhancing your results between clinic visits. Whether you are looking to address pigmentation, improve skin texture or simply invest in your long-term skin health, Obagi gives your skin the best possible foundation.
