What are they?

  • Dermal fillers and lip fillers are made of cross-linked hyaluronic acid (HA).
  • Hyaluronic acid is naturally made in our body and holds on to water to keep our skin hydrated and plump.
  • Filler HA is cross-linked so it stays around in the body for longer (from 6 months to 2 years).
  • There are many different types of brand on the market, all varying in quality and consistency, hence it is important to go to an experienced practitioner for the right product.
  • Note: Other types of dermal filler do exist, but the vast majority of "filler" is referring to HA filler. Always inform your practitioner if you have had filler previously.

What do they do?

  • Fillers are used to fill in areas that have lost volume over time and give a better shape to affected areas. Can be used to lift tissues, fill areas or mask lines.
  • Give a more plump and youthful appearance.
  • As we age, we lose muscle mass, fat pad density, skin becomes more lax and loose. As a result we get noticeable hollowed areas or tissues travel southward!
  • Fillers can be administered in most areas of the face and lips, but care is needed in specific areas.
