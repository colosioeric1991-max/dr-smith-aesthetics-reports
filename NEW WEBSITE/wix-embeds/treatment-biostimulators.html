What are biostimulators?

Biostimulators are injectable treatments that work by stimulating your body's own collagen production, rather than simply adding volume like traditional fillers. Results develop gradually and naturally over several weeks, improving skin quality, firmness and facial structure with a longer-lasting effect.

At Dr Smith Aesthetics we offer two biostimulator treatments: HarmonyCa® and Sculptra®. Both are carried out by Dr Smith following a full medical consultation to ensure suitability and create a personalised treatment plan.

HarmonyCa®  ·  1 hour

HarmonyCa® is an advanced hybrid injectable that combines hyaluronic acid (dermal filler) with calcium hydroxyapatite (a collagen-stimulating biostimulator). This unique combination provides both immediate lifting and contouring, while also encouraging your body's natural collagen production for longer-term skin rejuvenation.

The hyaluronic acid component provides instant volume and structural support, helping to enhance areas such as the cheeks and jawline. The calcium hydroxyapatite works over time to stimulate collagen production, improving skin quality, firmness and elasticity.

HarmonyCa® is commonly used to restore facial definition, improve mild to moderate skin laxity, soften the appearance of jowls, and create a more refreshed and supported appearance. Most patients require a single treatment session, with results typically lasting 12–18 months.

Benefits of HarmonyCa®:

  • Immediate lifting and contouring
  • Stimulates natural collagen production
  • Improves skin firmness and elasticity
  • Enhances cheek and jawline definition
  • Helps improve mild skin laxity and jowling
  • Long-lasting results (typically 12–18 months)

Sculptra®  ·  1 hour

Sculptra® is a PLLA (poly-L-lactic acid) biostimulator designed to gradually restore facial volume and improve skin quality over time. Unlike traditional dermal fillers, Sculptra® works by stimulating your body's own collagen production, helping to improve facial structure, skin firmness and overall facial rejuvenation with natural-looking results.

Common treatment areas include the cheeks, temples, jawline, lower face, and areas affected by age-related volume loss. Results develop gradually over several weeks and can continue to improve for several months as new collagen is produced.

Most patients require a course of 2–3 treatment sessions. The listed price includes 1 vial of Sculptra® per session. During your consultation, Dr Smith will assess your facial structure, skin quality and treatment goals to determine whether 1 or 2 vials per session are needed. If an additional vial is required, this will be discussed before treatment and charged at £475 per additional vial.

Benefits of Sculptra®:

  • Stimulates natural collagen production
  • Gradually restores facial volume
  • Improves skin firmness and texture
  • Natural-looking, long-lasting results
  • Suitable for facial rejuvenation and age-related volume loss

Who is suitable?

Biostimulators are particularly well suited to patients who want gradual, natural improvement rather than an immediate dramatic change. They are ideal for those looking to restore facial structure, address mild to moderate skin laxity, or improve overall skin quality with results that last.

A full medical consultation with Dr Smith is required before treatment to confirm suitability and agree a personalised plan.
