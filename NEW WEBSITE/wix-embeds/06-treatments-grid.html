Our Treatments

Our range of aesthetic treatments includes anti-wrinkle injections, fillers, skin boosters, polynucleotides, and PRP. Each treatment is carefully designed to enhance your natural beauty and boost your confidence.

Anti-Wrinkle

Anti-wrinkle injections are a popular treatment to reduce the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, giving you a smoother and more youthful complexion.

◈

Fillers

Fillers are used to add volume to areas of the face that have lost elasticity, providing a plump and rejuvenated look for a more youthful appearance.

◇

Skin Boosters

Skin boosters are designed to hydrate and nourish the skin from within, improving skin texture and elasticity for a radiant and glowing complexion.

◉

Polynucleotides

Poly-nucleotides are fragments of highly purified DNA that instruct your own skin cells to produce more collagen and elastin. Great results that improve over weeks.

❋

PRP & PRF

Blood is taken from your arm and centrifuged down. The plasma is rich in growth factors to stimulate collagen and elastin in skin, and increase hair growth where lost.

⬡

Microneedling

Medical-grade microneedling using the Dermapen 4, one of the most advanced devices on the market. Can be combined with PRF, exosomes or polynucleotides for enhanced results.
