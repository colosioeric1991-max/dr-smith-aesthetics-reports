Meet Dr Smith

Dr Lee Smith, Medical Aesthetic Doctor, Kennington, South London

After earning a degree in Biomedical Science and then subsequently completing a degree in Medicine, Dr Smith gained extensive experience working within the NHS as a doctor across a variety of specialties.

Recognising his passion for aesthetics, he has now dedicated himself to working with patients to help achieve their aesthetic goals. He is committed to providing personalised care and tailored treatments, ensuring that each client feels confident and satisfied with their results.

He runs his own aesthetics clinic in South London, whilst also teaching botox, filler and skin rejuvenation to doctors, nurses and other medical professionals.
