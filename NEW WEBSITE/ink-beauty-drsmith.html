Dr Smith Aesthetics is led by Dr Lee Smith MBBS BSc (Hons), a fully qualified medical doctor with years of experience in aesthetic medicine. Practising from Ink Beauty Skin Clinic in Kennington, South London, Dr Smith Aesthetics combines clinical precision with an eye for natural, harmonious results.

Unlike many aesthetic clinics, every treatment is performed personally by Dr Lee Smith with no delegation. This ensures a consistently high standard of care and outcomes tailored to each individual patient.

The approach is holistic: thorough consultations, honest advice, and treatments designed to enhance your natural features without over-correction.

What Dr Smith
Aesthetics Offers

All treatments at Dr Smith Aesthetics are carried out personally by Dr Lee Smith at Ink Beauty Skin Clinic. Each one is tailored to your skin, your goals, and your anatomy with no one-size-fits-all approach.

Anti-Wrinkle
Injections

Botulinum toxin treatments to soften expression lines, forehead furrows, crow's feet, and more. Subtle, natural-looking results.

Dermal
Fillers

Hyaluronic acid fillers to restore volume, define contours, and rejuvenate the lips, cheeks, jawline, and tear trough area.

Polynucleotides
(PDRN)

Advanced skin regeneration using polynucleotide injections to stimulate collagen, reduce fine lines, and deeply hydrate the skin.

PRP &
PRF

Platelet-rich plasma and fibrin treatments using your body's own growth factors to promote skin renewal and hair restoration.

Microneedling

Collagen-induction therapy to improve skin texture, reduce scarring, tighten pores, and create a smoother, more even complexion.

Skin Boosters
& Profhilo

Deep hydration and bio-remodelling treatments that restore skin laxity, radiance, and elasticity from within. Ideal for face, neck, and hands.
