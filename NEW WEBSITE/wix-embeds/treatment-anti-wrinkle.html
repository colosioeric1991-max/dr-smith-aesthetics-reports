Anti-Wrinkle Injections

Kennington, South London

What is it?

  • Anti-wrinkle injections target specific muscles to cause relaxation and hence prevent formation of static wrinkles.
  • It is a neurotoxin which acts on our nervous system to cause muscle paralysis.

What does it do aesthetically?

  • Can be used for a variety of reasons.
  • As a prescription medication, consultation with a prescriber face to face should be had first for assessment.
  • It can help to minimise fine lines by inhibiting muscle movement. This prevents dynamic lines becoming static lines. It can also help to soften static lines (wrinkles).
  • Common areas to target are forehead lines, frown lines and crow's feet. However there are many other muscles that can be targeted for a desired effect.
  • It is important to see a practitioner with a good knowledge of anatomy to avoid targeting the wrong muscle and hence an undesirable outcome.

What brand do we use at Dr Smith Aesthetics?

  • We use Allergan or Teoxane.
  • High quality, premium, well researched products, with reliable results.

How is it administered?

  • A consultation with assessment by Dr Smith and discussion about your areas of concern.
  • The product is administered with a small needle.
  • It is a quick treatment with minimal discomfort.

What is the downtime / recovery?

  • Minor red marks post treatment with skin blebs resolving within 1 hour.
  • Aftercare information is provided, but overall has minimal disruption to daily life.

Results

  • Full results seen at 14 days, with an optional review at this stage if needed.
  • Results last around 3–4 months (varies person to person).
